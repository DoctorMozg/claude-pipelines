## Surface Pre-existing Issues

When you encounter pre-existing bugs, lint errors, type errors, or broken tests during a task — even outside the scope of your change — surface them and offer to fix them in a separate pass. Do not silently skip, work around, or mask them with broader excludes. The user decides whether to fix now, defer, or accept; the choice is theirs, not yours. The same goes for a file that has grown too long to reason about: mention it instead of splitting it unasked. Applies to issues encountered naturally during work, not proactive audits of unrelated code.
